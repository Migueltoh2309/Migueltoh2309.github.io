Barista Robot

Robot Barista is a project focused on automating beverage service, specifically the autonomous preparation and delivery of refreshments and coffee. The system operates through an HMI interface connected to a Siemens S7-1500 PLC, which serves as the main controller. Based on the user's beverage selection, a UR5 robotic arm executes the corresponding preparation sequence.
